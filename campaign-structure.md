# AshisDigitalHub worldwide launch structure

## Status

**Draft only — no paid campaign has been created or activated.** A live campaign requires the final ad account, billing method, destination URLs, and explicit approval to spend $10/day.

## Budget and objective

- Daily budget: **$10/day total**
- Objective: lead generation through the consultation form
- Primary conversion: successful `/api/leads` submission
- Secondary conversion: qualified consultation request
- Test window: 7 days before making a change
- Geo: worldwide, excluding locations that cannot be served or do not match the service language
- Language: English creative; add localized variants only after lead quality evidence

## Facebook / Instagram structure

| Level | Draft setup |
|---|---|
| Campaign | `ADH — Worldwide Leads — AI Automation — Test 01` |
| Objective | Leads / website conversion |
| Budget | $6/day campaign budget |
| Ad set A | Worldwide, broad targeting, age 18+, automatic placements |
| Ad set B | Worldwide, technology / creator / small-business interests, only if enough budget remains |
| Ad | 9:16 avatar video, 1:1 crop, primary text, headline, CTA `Learn More` |
| Destination | Landing page consultation section with UTM parameters |

With only $6/day, start with one broad ad set and two creative variants rather than splitting budget too thinly.

## Video script

**Opening:** “Still losing hours to scattered digital tasks?”

**Value:** “AshisDigitalHub sets up professional blog accounts, advertising videos, AI automation, and technical support in one practical system.”

**Outcome:** “Save time, protect your data, and keep your business moving.”

**CTA:** “Visit AshisDigitalHub and request your clear next step today.”

Use a calm, credible AI avatar, clean technical studio background, captions, high contrast, and no unsupported income promises. The claim is operational: save time and organize work, not guaranteed earnings.

## Tracking URLs

- Facebook/Instagram: `/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=adh_worldwide_leads_test01&utm_content=avatar_v1`
- YouTube: `/?utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=video&utm_campaign=adh_worldwide_video_test01&utm_content=avatar_v1`

## Review gates

Before activation, confirm: ad account ID, billing method, landing URL, privacy/consent copy, pixel or Google tag event, final video, daily cap, audience exclusions, and approval of the exact campaign names and $10/day spend.
